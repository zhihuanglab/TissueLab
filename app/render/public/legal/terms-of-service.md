## 2. Description of the Service

TissueLab provides software to view, organize, annotate, and analyze whole slide images ("WSIs") and related pathology data. The Service includes:

- **Web platform:** Hosted at tissuelab.org, for browsing WSIs stored on TissueLab servers.
- **Desktop application:** A local client allowing you to browse WSIs stored on your own computer or institutional network.
- **Public dataset access:** Curated access to publicly available WSI repositories such as TCGA, PathAI, and similar sources, subject to each source's own license.
- **Models and classifiers:** TissueLab provides foundation models, fine-tuned classifiers (e.g., linear probing, gradient-boosted heads on top of model checkpoints), and customized model-plus-classifier combinations.
- **Workflows:** Multi-step pipelines that combine models and parameters, either manually assembled by you or generated by TissueLab's agentic AI workflow builder.
