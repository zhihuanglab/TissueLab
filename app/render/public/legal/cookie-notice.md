---

## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

A **cookie** is a small text file that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, phone) when you visit. We also use related technologies that behave similarly:

- **`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`** — browser storage used to remember preferences and short-lived state.
- **Pixels / web beacons** — tiny invisible images that report when a page or email was opened.
- **SDKs** in the desktop app — embedded libraries that may transmit diagnostic information.
- **Server-side identifiers** — tokens we set in response headers to maintain your authenticated session.

For brevity, we refer to all of the above as "cookies" in this notice.

## 2. Categories of cookies we use

We classify cookies into three categories. The first is required for the Site to work; the others require your consent in jurisdictions where consent is mandatory (EU/EEA, UK, and certain US states). We do **not** use marketing or advertising cookies.

### 2.1 Strictly necessary (always on)
Required for core functionality such as authentication, security, load balancing, and remembering that you accepted this notice. You **cannot** turn these off without breaking the Site.

### 2.2 Functional
Remember non-essential preferences such as your selected theme, viewer zoom defaults, last-opened workspace, and language.

### 2.3 Analytics / performance
Help us understand how users interact with the Site so we can improve it — for example, which pages are visited, what errors occur, how long actions take. Aggregated; not used to advertise to you.

These analytics may overlap with the behavioral data described in our **Privacy Policy**. This Cookie Notice governs the *technical mechanism* (cookies in the browser); the Privacy Policy governs the *purposes* (product improvement and model training).

## 4. How to manage your choices

### 4.1 Cookie banner
On your first visit, you'll see a banner with three options:

- **Accept All** — enables every category.
- **Reject All (non-essential)** — keeps only strictly necessary cookies.
- **Manage Preferences** — toggle Functional and Analytics independently.

You can reopen the preference center anytime from the footer link **"Cookie Preferences"** at the bottom of every page.

### 4.2 Browser controls
Most browsers let you block or delete cookies from settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break the Site (you won't be able to log in).

### 4.3 Global Privacy Control (GPC)
We honor the GPC signal where required by law. If your browser sends GPC, we will treat it as an opt-out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

### 4.4 Do Not Track
We do not respond to legacy "Do Not Track" headers, which lack a consistent legal standard. Use GPC or our preference center instead.

### 4.5 Desktop application
The desktop app does not use browser cookies, but it may include analytics and crash-reporting SDKs (e.g., Sentry). You can disable analytics in **Settings → Privacy → Diagnostics** in the app.
