## 1. Defined layers

### 1.1 Foundation Model
A pre-trained neural network checkpoint that produces general-purpose representations of pathology images. Foundation Models on the Service fall into three sub-categories:

(a) **In-house Foundation Model** — developed by the TissueLab team; legal title held by Penn and administered through PCI.
(b) **Public Foundation Model** — third-party open-source or publicly released model, used under the terms of its applicable license (e.g., Apache-2.0, research-use-only).
(c) **Third-Party Proprietary Foundation Model** — licensed to TissueLab under a separate commercial agreement (not initially available on the Service).

### 1.2 Classifier
A model head trained on top of a Foundation Model's checkpoint, including:

(a) Linear classifiers ("linear probes") fitted on frozen embeddings.
(b) Gradient-boosted classifiers (e.g., XGBoost-style heads) trained on frozen embeddings.
(c) Other lightweight heads fitted on top of a frozen or partially-frozen Foundation Model.

A Classifier is typically created by fine-tuning to a specific downstream task (e.g., tumor subtype classification, biomarker prediction).

### 1.3 Customized Model
The combination of a Foundation Model and one or more Classifiers, packaged as a single deployable artifact on the Service.

### 1.4 Workflow
A multi-step pipeline that orchestrates one or more Models, Classifiers, Customized Models, pre-processing steps, post-processing steps, and parameters. Workflows include:

(a) **Manually-assembled Workflows** — constructed step-by-step by a user via the Service's workflow builder.
(b) **Agentic-AI Workflows** — generated by TissueLab's agentic AI workflow builder, which uses an LLM-driven planner to combine components into a functional pipeline given a task description.

## 3. Contributions and the "benefit of use, not ownership" principle

### 3.1
When a pathologist or other user contributes annotations, corrections, ratings, labels, or other supervisory signal that is used (under the consent rules in our Privacy Policy) to train, fine-tune, or evaluate a TissueLab Model, Classifier, Customized Model, or Workflow:

(a) The contribution is intended to **benefit the user's own research workflows** on TissueLab and the broader research community of TissueLab users.

(b) The contribution does **not** make the user a co-author, co-inventor, or co-owner of the resulting Model, Classifier, Customized Model, or Workflow.

(c) The resulting intellectual property is created by the TissueLab team; legal title is held solely by Penn and administered through PCI, including any patents that may issue on novel methods.

(d) Where a user's contribution is materially significant to a published research artifact (e.g., a model card, a benchmark, a paper), TissueLab will use reasonable efforts to credit the user appropriately, subject to the user's consent to be named.

### 3.2 License grant from user to TissueLab

You grant Penn — which holds legal title to the TissueLab team's work, administered through PCI — a **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable license** to:

(a) use your contributions and any derivatives thereof (including embeddings, gradients, and evaluation results) to train, fine-tune, evaluate, distribute, and commercialize TissueLab Models, Classifiers, Customized Models, and Workflows; and

(b) include your contributions in datasets retained for model lineage, reproducibility, audit, and regulatory purposes.

### 3.3 License from TissueLab to you
You receive the rights to use the resulting Models, Classifiers, Customized Models, and Workflows in accordance with the Terms of Service (Section 8) — the same rights as any other user. You do **not** receive any expanded right or ownership stake by virtue of having contributed.

### 3.4 Your fine-tuned models — download, use, and publication

When you fine-tune a model on the Service using your own annotations or data:

(a) **Ownership.** A fine-tuned model consists of (i) the **fine-tuned layer** you trained (e.g., a classifier head or adapter fitted on your data) and (ii) the underlying **base Foundation Model**. Legal title to the base Foundation Model and to all TissueLab components remains with the TissueLab team / Penn (legal title held by Penn, administered through PCI) or the respective third-party owner. You do not acquire ownership of the model by fine-tuning it.

(b) **What you may download, at any time:** (i) your own annotation data and User Content, and (ii) the **fine-tuned layer** you trained on your own data, in a portable format. ## 4. Agentic AI workflow ownership

### 4.1
When TissueLab's agentic AI workflow builder generates a Workflow:

(a) The generated **Workflow itself** (the orchestration plan, parameter choices, component selections, and any prompt scaffolding) is created by the TissueLab team; legal title is held by Penn and administered through PCI.

(b) The fact that you provided the **task description** that triggered the generation does not transfer ownership of the resulting Workflow to you.

(c) You receive a license to **use** the generated Workflow within the Service for your research, per the Terms of Service.

### 4.2
Improvements to the agentic AI workflow builder informed by your usage are created by the TissueLab team; legal title is held by Penn and administered through PCI.

## 6. Research collaborations and joint IP

If TissueLab and an institution wish to undertake a **joint research project** that may produce co-authored intellectual property (e.g., a new model architecture co-designed by both teams), the parties shall negotiate a separate written research collaboration agreement specifying:

- Background IP of each party
- Scope of the project
- Allocation of new IP (including co-ownership, exclusive license-back, or assignment)
- Publication rights and timing
- Funding and contributions
- Term, termination, and survival
