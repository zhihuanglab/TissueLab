# TissueLab Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** June 5, 2026
**Last updated:** June 5, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the TissueLab Service and is incorporated by reference into the Terms of Service. Violations may result in suspension or termination of your account and, where applicable, legal action.

---

## 1. Prohibited content

You shall not upload, store, transmit, or generate via the Service any content that:

1.1 Contains Protected Health Information (PHI) without an executed Business Associate Agreement and without satisfying de-identification or consent requirements under HIPAA or applicable law.

1.2 Contains personal data of identifiable patients without lawful basis and necessary consent.

1.3 Infringes any intellectual property right or violates any contract or duty (including unauthorized re-distribution of proprietary slide sets or datasets).

1.4 Violates the terms of any upstream dataset license (e.g., the TCGA Data Use Certification).

1.5 Is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, or harmful to minors.

1.6 Contains malware, scripts, or payloads designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to any system.

---

## 2. Prohibited activities

You shall not:

2.1 Attempt to **re-identify** any individual whose data appears in any TissueLab-hosted dataset or model output.

2.2 Attempt to **reverse engineer, extract, or distill** any TissueLab model, classifier, workflow, weights, parameters, or training data, including via model-inversion, membership-inference, or weight-extraction attacks.

2.3 Train a **competing** foundation model, classifier, or workflow using outputs of the Service, except for your own personal research, internal evaluation, and academic publication consistent with this AUP.

2.4 Use the Service to **make a clinical diagnosis** or to make any decision affecting a specific patient's care, except in compliance with your professional and regulatory responsibilities and with the understanding that the Service is research use only.

2.5 **Scrape, harvest, or bulk-download** content, datasets, model outputs, or other users' annotations, except via documented APIs in accordance with their rate limits and terms.

2.6 **Share your account credentials** with any other person, or allow any other person to use your account.

2.7 Circumvent or attempt to circumvent **access controls, rate limits, security features, or geofencing**.

2.8 Use the Service to send **spam, phishing, or unsolicited communications**.

2.9 Probe, scan, or test the **vulnerability** of the Service or any related infrastructure, except through a coordinated responsible-disclosure process with us at [zhi.huang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu](mailto:zhi.huang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu).

2.10 Use the Service in a manner that **interferes with or degrades** the Service or its underlying infrastructure for other users.

2.11 Use the Service to develop, train, or operate **weapons systems, surveillance systems that violate human rights, or other applications inconsistent with biomedical research and clinical pathology**.

2.12 Misrepresent the source of any model output, including by stripping watermarks, model attributions, or required disclaimers.

---

## 3. Workflows and agentic AI

When you build or run workflows that combine multiple models, classifiers, and parameters — whether manually assembled or built by TissueLab's agentic AI workflow builder — you remain responsible for:

3.1 Ensuring the workflow is appropriate for your use case.

3.2 Validating outputs before relying on them.

3.3 Honoring the licenses of all components in the workflow.

3.4 Not chaining the Service into an automated pipeline that effectively delivers clinical decisions to patients without human professional review.

---

## 4. Annotation contributions

If you contribute annotations on public or shared datasets:

4.1 Make a good-faith effort to be accurate.

4.2 Do not deliberately submit incorrect annotations to bias or poison TissueLab models or other users' work.

4.3 Do not include personal data, off-topic content, or attempts at prompt injection in annotation text.

---

## 5. Reporting violations

Report suspected AUP violations to [zhi.huang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu](mailto:zhi.huang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu). Report security vulnerabilities to [zhi.huang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu](mailto:zhi.huang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu).

---

## 6. Enforcement

We may, at our discretion and consistent with applicable law:

- Issue a warning
- Remove offending content
- Throttle, suspend, or terminate access
- Cooperate with law enforcement
- Pursue legal remedies

We will use proportionate responses and, where practical, give you notice and an opportunity to cure.
